Puzzle 2: Guess the Rule

Enter three numbers. The page will tell you whether your sequence matches the hidden rule.

Example that matches: 2, 4, 6

Guess history

    Rule Explanation

    The hidden rule is: the three numbers must be in strictly increasing order.

    For a sequence written as A, B, C, it matches only when A < B < C. This means each number must be greater than the number before it. Equal numbers do not count, and decreasing numbers do not count. Whether the numbers are even or odd does not matter. Any set of numbers where B is greater than A and C is greater than B satisfies the rule. Did you get it right?
