Logic and Rule Testing

You will see two puzzles. In each puzzle, select only the item or items that must be checked to verify the rule. The page will not tell you whether you are right until the final explanation.

Puzzle 1: The Cards

Each card has a letter on one side and a number on the opposite side. You can see only one side of each card.

Rule to verify: If there is an A on a card, then there is a 7 on the other side.

Select every card that must be flipped over to determine whether the rule has been broken.

No feedback yet. Your answer will be evaluated after both puzzles.

Puzzle 2: The Bar

There are four people in a bar. In order to consume alcoholic beverages, individuals must be 21 years old or more.

Rule to verify: If a person is drinking alcohol, then that person is at least 21 years old.

Select every individual you would need to investigate to determine whether the rule is being broken.

No feedback yet. The explanation will reveal the correct answers.

Explanation and Correct Answers

Puzzle 1 answer: flip A and 8.

The A card must be checked because it could violate the rule if the other side is not 7. The 8 card must be checked because if the other side is A, then the rule has been broken.

You do not need to check G because the rule says nothing about cards that do not have A. You do not need to check 7 because the rule does not say that every 7 must have an A on the other side; it only says that every A must have a 7.

Puzzle 2 answer: investigate the 12-year-old and the beer drinker.

The beer drinker must be checked to confirm they are at least 21. The 12-year-old must be checked to confirm they are not drinking alcohol.

You do not need to check the 35-year-old because anyone 21 or older is allowed to drink alcohol under the rule. You do not need to check the water drinker because drinking water cannot violate a rule about alcohol consumption.

Why these are the same puzzle

Both puzzles test the same conditional rule structure: If P, then Q. To test that kind of rule, you check P and not Q. You do not need to check Q or not P.

P A Drinking beer / alcohol
Q 7 Age 21 or older
not Q 8 12 years old
not P G Drinking water
